Daily Archive : 1,500+

A running record rather than a body of finished works. Oil pastel on paper, variable dimensions, begun in 2019 and still running. Below it, a year-by-year record.

Drawn without selection and kept: some days leave several sheets, some leave one, some leave none. The archive holds the attention of the day rather than the finished painting — dense where a period was loud, sparse where it was quiet.

It runs alongside the paintings. A drawing may become a structure, a colour, or nothing at all.
