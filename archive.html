(04)  Archive

Daily Archive : 1,500+

A performative archive rather than a body of separate works. Oil pastel on paper, variable dimensions, begun in 2019 and still running.

2019 — 2026 1,500+ sheets Ongoing

Without selection, drawn steadily over time: some days leave several sheets, some leave none. The archive keeps the attention of the moment rather than the finished painting, reading as a record of looking — dense where the period was loud, sparse where it was quiet.

It runs alongside the paintings and feeds them: a drawing may become a structure, a colour, or nothing at all.
