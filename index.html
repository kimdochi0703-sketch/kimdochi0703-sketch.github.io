E.ZY — Lee Ji Hwan

Seoul ↓

Little by little we are handing our judgment to somewhere else.

Dozens of times a day we ask, choose and decide, and at every turn there is already a screen we cannot see, a calculation already made. The question is still a person's to ask; the hand that settles the answer is slowly moving outside us. Consigned Consciousness keeps that movement on the screen. The circles and rectangles inside it, and the lines that join them, look like passages running both ways, but they mark a direction that opens outward.

In Frame that interest extends to a structure that is invisible and still confines us. The frame does not show itself, and we guess at it only from the contours left on the surface. Each time one screen divides into planes large and small, every piece becomes a universe of its own. Believing we can go anywhere, we end up moving inside that divided place.

Behind both bodies of work stand the paintings of 2023–24. Before line and structure took hold of the surface, the years were spent rubbing and layering oil pastel until form scattered into particles, with nothing but the sense of the hand to hold on to.
