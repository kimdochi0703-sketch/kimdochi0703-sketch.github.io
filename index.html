Little by little we hand our judgment to somewhere else.

I paint. What I want on the canvas is the point where a person's asking, feeling and deciding quietly leaves the person and settles into a structure that answers instead.

Consigned Consciousness began there. We ask, choose and decide dozens of times a day, and much of that already passes through a screen and an algorithm. We are still the ones asking; less and less are we the ones forming the answer. The series paints that scene.

A circle is where thought and feeling sit. A rectangle is the screen that receives them. The line between the two looks like a road that runs both ways, but it marks a direction: what goes out.

In Frame the frame itself is never seen. We know it only through the contours it leaves on the surface. Divide one plane into rectangles of different sizes and each becomes a universe of its own. We believe we move freely; we stay inside that universe.

Behind the recent paintings are earlier ones moving the other way: oil pastel rubbed and layered until form loosens into particles.
