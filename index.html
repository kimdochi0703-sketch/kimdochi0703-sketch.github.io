(01)  Statement

The line that holds a structure up is also the line that holds a painting.

Seoul

Artist note

I move between painting and structural work: the dry drafting line of architecture, a circle, a rectangle, and the thin trajectory drawn between them.

In Consigned Consciousness a circle is the vessel of thought and perception; a rectangle is the display and the artificial structure that absorb our data. The line joining the two traces what is handed over — information taken in through the screen, consciousness sent back out to the system.

The series reached No. 23 in November 2026. Beside it a set of individual works has gathered through the year — frames, supports, timelines, one-eyed portraits — and the earlier paintings move the other way: oil pastel smeared until form loosens into particles.

In Frame the canvas stops being a surface that carries an image and becomes the condition that allows an image to appear at all.
