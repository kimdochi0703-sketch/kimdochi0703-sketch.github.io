E.ZY

Lee Ji Hwan

b. 1993, Seoul. Lives and works in Seoul.

E.ZY works between painting and structure. He studied architecture engineering and furniture design, and has painted since. The current work follows what a person gives away when they look, ask and decide through a screen.

Consigned Consciousness began in 2026 and is still running. The structural work Frame and the paintings of frames and supports push the same question into the object. Beneath all of it is a drawing archive begun in 2019.
