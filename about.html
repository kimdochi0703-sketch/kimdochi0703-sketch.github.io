E.ZY works across painting, structural work and drawing. Trained in architecture engineering and furniture design, he treats the canvas as a building surface: drafted lines, pressed linen, and the tension between what is seen and what holds it.

Recent work follows how human attention is handed to algorithmic structures, and returns to the older question of why anything exists at all.
