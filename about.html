E.ZY

Lee Ji Hwan

Lives and works in Seoul.

E.ZY works in painting and in structure. He studied architectural engineering and furniture design, and has painted ever since. The current work follows what a person gives away when they look, ask and decide through a screen.

Delegated Consciousness began in 2026 and is still running. The paintings of frames and supports, and the structural work Frame, carry the same question into an object. Beneath all of it is a drawing archive begun in 2019.
